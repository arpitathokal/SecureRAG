Password Policy

Passwords must contain at least fourteen characters. Multi-factor authentication is required for access to production systems.

Incident Reporting

Suspected security incidents must be reported to the security team within one hour of discovery. Reports should include the affected system and a concise description, but must not include passwords or authentication tokens.

Data Classification

Information is classified as Public, Internal, Confidential, or Restricted. Restricted information requires encryption in transit and at rest.
